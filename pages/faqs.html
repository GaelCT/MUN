Frequently Asked Questions


No experience is required. CSUB MUN welcomes students of all majors and experience levels. We provide training and support to help new members succeed.

Members represent countries in simulated United Nations committees, research global issues, write position papers, and participate in debates, and negotiations at conferences.

Generally, members attend meetings every Friday for about 90 minutes.

Members develop public speaking, research, negotiation, critical thinking, and writing skills, as well as conference and professionalism.

All CSUB students are welcome to join, regardless of major.

You can join by attending one of our meetings. Meeting information can be found on our Instagram page @csubmun
